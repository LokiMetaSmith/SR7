# SRX: eXtream Edition Ruleset

This module provides the official ruleset addition to bridge the streamlined, fixed-threshold ruleset of Shadowrun X (SRX) with the chaotic, high-variance "Merged World" lore of Shadowrun 7th Edition (SR7).

> **WARNING TO GAME MASTERS**
> These rules significantly increase the lethality, variance, and systemic interconnectedness of the game. The introduction of Wild Dice and G.O.D. Manifestations can cause encounters to spiral quickly. It is highly recommended to clearly communicate these risks to your players before implementing this module.

## 1. The Wild Dice System

**Wild Dice** are a new category of dice rolled alongside the 2 standard SRX Crit Dice and the remaining standard dice pool. They represent the volatile, unpredictable nature of the Merged World.

*   **Explosion:** A result of **6** on a Wild Die explodes, immediately generating **3 hits**.
*   **Cancellation:** A result of **1** on a Wild Die actively cancels out **all normal 5s** rolled within the non-wild portion of the current dice pool.

## 2. Unified Saturation (Replacing Noise & Background Count)

**The Gaiasphere/Matrix Merge:** The boundaries between the physical world, the Astral Plane, and the Matrix have collapsed. The environment is now thoroughly saturated with ambient, programmable magical nanites.

*   **Saturation Level:** SRX's "Medium/Heavy" Noise mechanics are removed. The environment is now measured by a unified **Saturation Level** ranging from 1 to 6.
*   **Forced Volatility:** Whenever a character makes a **Sorcery, Conjuring, Mysticism, Hacking, Software, or Threading test**, they **MUST** replace a number of standard dice in their pool with **Wild Dice** equal to the current Saturation Level.

## 3. Digital Essence & The Oil/Water Paradigm

The distinction between digital consciousness and spiritual life force has been eradicated.

*   **Digital Essence:** Sprites, AIs, and all complex Matrix entities now possess an **Essence of 6** and a fully targetable **Astral Aura**.
*   **Essence Drain & Death:** These digital entities can be targeted by magical Essence Drain. If their Essence reaches **0**, their core code irrevocably fragments, and the entity dies.
*   **The Oil/Water Paradigm:** Magic and Resonance are fundamentally, mechanically mutually exclusive. **No entity can ever possess both attributes simultaneously.**

## 4. Unified Possession & Biofeedback

Whether it involves a Rigger diving into a drone's control systems, a Spirit possessing a metahuman vessel, or a user being overridden by an experimental BTL chip, the mechanical resolution is identical.

*   **Attribute Override:** The possessing/controlling entity completely overrides the host's **Mental Attributes (WIL, LOG, INT, CHA)**.
*   **Host Capabilities:** The possessing/controlling entity utilizes the host's **Physical Attributes (BOD, AGI, REA)**.
*   **Biofeedback:** If the physical host takes Physical damage, the controlling entity immediately suffers **half that damage (rounded down)** as **Stun Biofeedback**. This Biofeedback damage is resisted using the controlling entity's **Willpower + Body**.
*   **Violent Ejection:** If the physical host is destroyed (e.g., reduced to 0 or fewer Health/Structure points), the controlling entity is violently ejected. The entity immediately takes **6 unresisted Stun damage** and suffers a **-2 dice pool penalty** to all actions for **10 minutes**.

## 5. G.O.D. Manifestations

The Grid Overwatch Division (G.O.D.) no longer relies solely on virtual Black IC. The environment itself enforces the law.

*   **Physical Manifestation:** The SRX hacking failure rules are updated. If a Hacker rolls a **1 on a Wild Die** while making an **opposed Hacking test**, the local ambient nanites instantly consume nearby matter.
*   **The Antibody:** This process immediately spawns a physical **G.O.D. Antibody** (functioning mechanically as a dual-natured Sprite/Spirit hybrid) directly into physical combat with the offending Hacker.
