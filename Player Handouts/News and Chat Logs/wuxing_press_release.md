# WUXING INCORPORATED: PRESS RELEASE

**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**
**Date:** 2078-10-18
**Subject:** Security Enhancements and Environmental Restoration in the Zkazena District

**HONG KONG / ZKAZENA** – Wuxing Incorporated today announced a comprehensive expansion of its security and environmental restoration efforts within the Zkazena district, specifically focusing on the area surrounding the historic White Tower facility and the former Tar Creek mining site.

In recent weeks, localized atmospheric and electromagnetic anomalies have been recorded in the sector. Wuxing wishes to assure the public that these are entirely natural phenomena, exacerbated by the historical environmental degradation of the area prior to Wuxing’s stewardship.

To ensure the safety of our personnel and the broader Zkazena community, Wuxing has contracted the highly decorated **Fuchsia Dragons** private security firm. The Fuchsia Dragons will assist White Tower security forces in maintaining public order, securing hazardous zones, and expediting the delivery of essential corporate services.

"The deployment of the Fuchsia Dragons is a proactive measure," stated Director Lin Hao of Wuxing's Regional Operations. "Their expertise in urban stabilization will allow our researchers to focus entirely on their vital work—which includes groundbreaking advancements in bioremediation and cellular longevity treatments that will benefit all metahumanity."

Furthermore, Wuxing has established a temporary "Containment Zone" protocol around the Tar Creek perimeter. This is a standard safety measure to prevent unauthorized access to geologically unstable terrain during our intensive cleanup operations. Citizens are strongly advised to adhere to all posted warnings and corporate security directives.

Wuxing remains committed to transforming the Zkazena district into a beacon of progress and environmental recovery.
