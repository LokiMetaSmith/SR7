# GM Campaign Guide: Hollow Resonance (Necessity Knows No Law - Part Two)

This guide adapts the "Hollow Resonance" storyline into a playable Shadowrun campaign. It expands on the themes of existential horror, corporate exploitation, and the blurring lines between life, death, and data.

## Campaign Overview
**Setting:** Zkazena (Crispyopolis), a ruined company town in a localized magic "Containment Zone".
**Themes:** Transhumanism, Grief, Extralegal survival, the Horror of Immortality.
**Core Conflict:** The players are caught in a multi-faction war over "Spark+," a child who has merged with an alien entity and the digitized ghost of a dead technomancer. Spark+ has the power to either stabilize the expanding Containment Zone or reshape reality itself.

---

## Part 1: Lore and Background

### Places
*   **Crispyopolis (Zkazena):** A blighted former mercury-mining town now dominated by ghouls, toxic spirits, and the constant threat of HMHVV. Wuxing indirectly controls the area due to a secret Leónization (immortality) facility.
*   **The White Tower:** A 1500ft tall, heavily shielded corporate arcology dominating Zkazena. It houses the Leónization facility and sits atop a powerful, ancient ley line node.
*   **The Containment Zone:** An area of ultra-high background magic surrounding the old mines, Citadel, and White Tower. It warps reality and mutates those within it.
*   **Undercity & Necropolis:** Subterranean areas beneath the White Tower. The Undercity houses the ghoul workers for the Leónization process. The Necropolis contains ancient, powerful entities (like Nosferatu) held in stasis by the ley lines.
*   **The Citadel:** An ancient, unmapped area of extreme magic, home to dormant Obsidimen tied to a Liferock.

### Factions
*   **Wuxing Incorporated:** The megacorp controlling Zkazena. They seek to exploit the area's magic for Leónization and will burn the city (literally, via orbital bombardment) to cover their tracks if containment fails.
*   **White Tower Security & AI ("Oracle"):** The local defense force. Oracle is a cold, logic-driven AI that will lock down the Tower and sacrifice everyone inside to prevent the Zone from expanding.
*   **"New Beginnings" Life Insurance (Francky):** A predatory outfit using the "Resonator" (stolen Wuxing tech + magic) to create "Hollows"—imperfect resurrections of the dead—by tapping into the Zone's energy.
*   **Sister Sinalma (Bio-Arcane Division):** A rogue Wuxing researcher obsessed with using the Zone's power (via Spark+) to transcend metahumanity, viewing the disaster as an evolutionary chrysalis.
*   **Fuchsia Dragons:** A heavily armed mercenary company possessed by centipede insect spirits. They arrive under the guise of saving the city but actually intend to occupy it and control the Zone's power.
*   **The Local Ghouls (led by scav-kings like Ratchet):** The downtrodden majority. When the Zone surges, they are driven into a magically-amplified frenzy, becoming a massive horde.

### Key NPCs
*   **Kyber:** A jaded operative with CFD (multiple personality disorder: Observer, Enforcer, Negotiator). The reluctant guardian of Spark.
*   **Logi Prime:** A permanently nanite-infused Technomancer who sacrifices themselves to stop Francky's Resonator, their essence becoming trapped in a "crystalise."
*   **Logi_v2.1 (The Save State):** A backup copy of Logi downloaded into a fresh nanite body. They lack Logi Prime's recent memories (the "Grief Gap") and operate with cold, utilitarian logic.
*   **Spark:** A young boy used as the "battery" for the Resonator. He possesses a unique resonance.
*   **Spark+ (The Architect):** The merged entity of Spark, the alien "Wisp," and the ghost of Logi Prime. A being of immense, unstable reality-warping power.
*   **Cryptolock:** A ghoul rocker and diehard Zkazena resident who aids Kyber.
*   **Francky:** The slick, predatory salesman of "New Beginnings."
*   **Sister Sinalma:** The chillingly serene, dangerously augmented Bio-Arcane researcher.
*   **Captain Lazlow:** The massive, spirit-possessed leader of the Fuchsia Dragons.

## Part 2: Campaign Structure (Scene by Scene)

### Act 1: Better Off Dead
*The players are hired to investigate the "Hollows"—dead citizens returning to Zkazena.*

**Scene 1.1: Arrival in Zkazena**
*   **Hook:** A local fixer (or Kyber, acting as a Mr. Johnson) hires the runners to investigate the resurrections.
*   **Action:** The players navigate the toxic outskirts of Crispyopolis. They encounter wary ghouls and the oppressive "static" of ambient nanites and magic.
*   **Encounter:** A minor skirmish with territorial ghouls or scavengers near "The Last Shaft" noodle bar to establish the setting's danger.
*   **Investigation:** The players must gather intel on the resurrections. They learn about Grima's sister (Asa) returning as a "Hollow" and hear rumors of Francky and "New Beginnings."

**Scene 1.2: The Sales Pitch**
*   **Action:** The players track Francky down. He may try to pitch them his "Life Insurance."
*   **Check:** *Judge Intentions / Perception.* Players can spot the traumatized boy (Spark) with Francky and realize Francky is manipulating the locals' grief.

**Scene 1.3: The Resonator (Climax of Act 1)**
*   **Location:** St. Jude's Church (abandoned).
*   **Action:** The players raid Francky's operation. They discover the "Resonator" creating Hollows by tearing holes in the local Matrix/mana field, using Spark as a battery.
*   **Combat:** The players fight Francky (who uses strong illusions/magic) and his guards (or newly minted Hollows acting as meat shields).
*   **The Sacrifice:** The Resonator overloads. A player (or an NPC like Logi Prime) must make a lethal sacrifice to shut it down, plunging their hands into the core.
*   **Resolution:** The Resonator is destroyed. Francky escapes. The players recover the unconscious Spark and the "crystalise" (containing the Wisp and the sacrificed character's ghost).

### Act 2: Waking Dreams
*The players hide with Spark and the crystalise. They must deal with the psychological fallout and uncover the true threat.*

**Scene 2.1: The Sync Error (Roleplay Focus)**
*   **Location:** The Subway Hub (safehouse).
*   **Action:** If a player character sacrificed themselves, they return via a backup clone/Save State (arranged prior or provided by a mysterious faction). If an NPC (Logi) died, Logi_v2.1 arrives.
*   **Roleplay:** Emphasize the "Grief Gap." The new character/NPC doesn't share recent memories or secrets. The crystalise (Spark+) occasionally broadcasts the *real*, dead character's memories/warnings. The players must investigate salvaged data from the Resonator, uncovering "Project Nightingale" and Sister Sinalma's involvement.

**Scene 2.2: The Sky Bleeds (Climax of Act 2)**
*   **Action:** Spark+ begins having premonitions ("The sky bleeds violet").
*   **Event:** A massive, silent violet tear opens in the sky. The Containment Zone violently expands, swallowing Sector 4.
*   **Skill Checks:** *Survival / Athletics / Willpower.* The players must survive the initial reality-warping shockwave.
*   **The Horde:** The ambient magic spike drives the city's ghoul population totally insane. A massive horde begins swarming toward the White Tower.

### Act 3: The Siege of White Tower
*Trapped between the horde and corporate crossfire, the players must breach the White Tower.*

**Scene 3.1: Running the Gauntlet**
*   **Action:** The players must escort Spark+ through the chaotic, ghoul-infested streets towards the White Tower's shielding.
*   **Combat:** Fighting through waves of frenzied ghouls. The environment itself is hazardous (warped gravity, toxic magic flares).

**Scene 3.2: Enter the Dragons**
*   **Event:** The Fuchsia Dragons arrive in heavily armored hover-tanks.
*   **Action:** The players realize the Dragons are shooting *everyone*, not just ghouls. They must avoid the mercenary crossfire and slip into a secondary access hatch of the White Tower.
*   **Choice:** Do they try to fight the Dragons, or sneak past?

### Act 4: The Architect's Blueprint
*Inside the locked-down Tower, the players face the AI, Wuxing's betrayal, and rival factions.*

**Scene 4.1: The Blackout**
*   **Location:** White Tower Security Substation.
*   **Action:** The Tower AI ("Oracle") initiates a total Matrix blackout and lockdown.
*   **Discovery:** The players (or Logi_v2.1) discover Wuxing is preparing "Thor Shots" (orbital kinetic bombardment) to wipe Zkazena off the map.
*   **The Plan:** They realize Spark+ can act as a grounding rod at the Tower's central Ley Line Node to stabilize the Zone and stop the bombardment.

**Scene 4.2: The Three-Way Standoff**
*   **Action:** Sister Sinalma and Francky ambush the players in the substation, demanding Spark+.
*   **Combat:** A chaotic fight. Sinalma uses high-level cyber/magic barriers; Francky uses dirty tricks.
*   **Escape:** The players must break contact and sprint for the sub-basement Node with Spark+, leaving someone behind to hold the line if necessary.

### Act 5: Apotheosis (The Climax)
*The final confrontation at the Ley Line Node as the orbital strike descends.*

**Scene 5.1: The Central Node**
*   **Location:** The Sub-Basement (massive cavern over ancient ley lines).
*   **Action:** The players reach the Node. They have minutes before the orbital strike.
*   **The Choice:**
    *   **Stabilize:** Allow Spark+ to interface with the Node. This will likely "overwrite" the boy's consciousness, turning him into an eternal warden (a massive sacrifice).
    *   **Escape:** Try to flee the Tower with Spark+ before the strike, abandoning the city to destruction.
    *   **Fight:** Sinalma/Francky arrive for a final, desperate battle over the boy while the countdown ticks.

**Scene 5.2: The Merge and the Strike**
*   **Event:** The crystalise merges fully with Spark, creating the true "Architect" (Spark+).
*   **Action:** If the players allow it, Spark+ touches the Node.
*   **The Cataclysm:** The Thor Shot hits. Instead of destroying the city, Spark+ absorbs the kinetic and magical energy, creating a localized singularity.
*   **Resolution:** The White Tower is vaporized into a glass crater. The Containment Zone is neutralized. Spark+ survives, but is fundamentally changed into a powerful, god-like entity ("The seed is planted"). The players survive, but the world is forever altered.

## GM Notes: Running the Campaign
*   **The "Save State" Mechanic:** If a player character dies, you can offer them the "Save State" option. They return with a fresh body but lose all Karma/memories gained during the campaign up to that point. This enforces the "Grief Gap" mechanically and roleplay-wise.
*   **Magic in the Zone:** Once the Zone expands in Act 2, impose wild magic surges. Spells might over-cast automatically, cause unexpected environmental effects, or attract toxic spirits.
*   **Moral Ambiguity:** Emphasize that there are no "good guys." Wuxing wants to glass the city; Sinalma wants forced evolution; the Dragons want territory. The players must choose the lesser evil or forge their own bloody path.
