\section*{I. Core Mechanics: Dice \& Tests}

All actions use a streamlined success-counting system.

\begin{itemize}
\setlength{\itemsep}{0pt}\setlength{\parskip}{0pt}
\item
  \textbf{DICE POOL:} Attribute + Skill.
\item
  \textbf{\textsc{hits}:} All tests use six-sided dice (D6). A result of
  \textbf{5 or 6} is a \textsc{hit}.
\item
  \textbf{\textsc{glitches}:} If more than half the dice rolled are \textbf{1s},
  you \textsc{glitch} (a complication occurs). If you \textsc{glitch} with 0 hits, it's a
  \textbf{\color{sraccent}CRITICAL GLITCH} (catastrophe).
\item
  \textbf{Rule of 6 (Ro6):} When applicable (e.g., using Edge), a rolled
  6 counts as a \textsc{hit} and is rolled again.
\item
  \textbf{Wild Dice:} Used in high-mana or nanite-saturated areas. Roll
  these with a different colored die. A \textbf{6} counts as \textbf{3
  \textsc{hits}} (and explodes if Ro6 applies). However, a \textbf{1} cancels all
  standard \textbf{5s} rolled in that pool.
\end{itemize}

\begin{jackpoint}{Glitch\_Walker}
Watch your back, chummer. The nanite density in OKC is spiking again. The streets have changed. Nanites in the rain, spirits in the code. Bring your Wild Dice or don't bother jacking in.
\end{jackpoint}

\begin{gmadvice}[Rules Reminder: GLITCHES]
Remember that a \textsc{glitch} doesn't always mean failure. A character can succeed at a task but still suffer a complication! Only a \textsc{critical glitch} means a complete catastrophe.
\end{gmadvice}

\subsection*{The Matrix \& The Gaiasphere}

The Matrix is no longer separate from reality; it is a nanite-infused
environment. Even the hermit in the woods is lit up with an AR signature
because the water and air are crawling with nanites.

\textbf{No Cold Sim (The Abyss Looks Back):} Cold Sim is dead. Any
active skill involving the Matrix requires a Direct Neural Interface
(\textsc{dni}), and all \textsc{dni} connections are Hot Sim. You put your brain and
soul on the line. Acting in AR without \textsc{dni} limits your dice pools to
the flat stats of your commlink or cyberdeck.

\section*{Metatype Karma Cost Formulas}

To ensure consistency and allow automated tools (like \texttt{balance\_generator.py}) to function properly, the following mathematical formulas serve as the baseline for calculating costs in this ruleset.

\begin{jackpoint}{Loki}
Keep in mind, chummers: these formulas are the hardcoded foundation. If you try to manually tweak a metatype without running the math, the whole drekking system might crash on you during character generation.
\end{jackpoint}

\subsection*{Base Cost Calculation}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item Calculate the sum of the maximum limits for all 9 core Attributes (\textsc{bod}, \textsc{agi}, \textsc{rea}, \textsc{str}, \textsc{wil}, \textsc{log}, \textsc{int}, \textsc{cha}, \textsc{edg}).
    \item Subtract 56 from this total.
    \item Multiply the difference by \textbf{15 Karma}. \textit{(If the result is negative, the base cost is 0.)}
\end{enumerate}
